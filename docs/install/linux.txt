DELAY LAMA - LINUX

1. Download the tar.gz matching your DAW's architecture: x86_64 or aarch64.
   Extract it. These builds use Ubuntu 24.04 and require X11 (or XWayland)
   and Vulkan.
2. Close your DAW. Choose a format it supports; you do not need all three.
   Copy the complete item, including everything inside a plug-in folder:

   Item                 Destination folder
   Delay Lama.vst3      ~/.vst3
   Delay Lama.clap      ~/.clap
   delay-lama.lv2       ~/.lv2

   The ~ means your home folder. Create the destination if it does not exist.
   Folders beginning with a dot are normally hidden in file managers.
3. Reopen your DAW, rescan its plug-ins, and load Delay Lama on an instrument
   track. Play MIDI notes or use its XY pad.

Do not rename the plug-in or copy only the .so file from inside its folder.
When updating, replace the whole old plug-in rather than merging its contents.
Keep accompanying documentation when redistributing this download.
Project information and redistribution terms: Documentation/README.md.
