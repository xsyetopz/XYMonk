DELAY LAMA - macOS

1. Open the DMG. The same universal download supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
2. Close your DAW. Choose a format it supports; you do not need all of them.
   Copy the complete item to the corresponding destination:

   Item                   Destination folder
   Delay Lama.vst3        ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   Delay Lama.clap        ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP
   Delay Lama.component   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components  (AUv2)
   delay-lama.lv2         ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/LV2

   In Finder, press Command-Shift-G and paste the destination. The ~ means
   your home folder. Create the final folder if it does not exist.
3. Eject the DMG. Reopen your DAW, rescan its plug-ins, and load Delay Lama
   on an instrument track. Play MIDI notes or use its XY pad.

AUv3 alternative:
Copy Delay Lama.app to /Applications and open that installed copy once before
reopening your DAW. This app contains the AUv3 extension; it is not a standalone
synthesizer. Keep it installed. Do not extract or move the .appex inside it.

The DMG is a download container, not an installer. Copy the plug-in before
ejecting it. Do not rename bundles or use Show Package Contents to install them.
When updating, replace the whole old plug-in rather than merging its contents.
Keep accompanying documentation when redistributing this download.
Project information and redistribution terms: Documentation/README.md.
